# V1 reward contribution policy and routing design

The owner approved pledging all verified StonkFun rewards paid in PUMP from an enrolled wallet, including rewards generated by other coins. Purchased PUMP, existing PUMP and ordinary transfers must remain protected. Rewards already paid while waiting for activation or while collection is paused must never be collected later.

This is the accepted policy and a proposed implementation design. The current `sweep` instruction still collects balance above an enrollment baseline and does not implement this policy. The routing interface below is not an existing StonkFun API or a deployed instruction. Integration support, distributor identity and treasury reward eligibility require StonkFun confirmation before implementation can be finalized or fundraising enabled.

## Accepted policy

| Funds or state | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| PENIS or any other holder asset except eligible PUMP rewards | Never transferred by contribution collection |
| PUMP held before enrollment | Keep with the holder |
| PUMP bought later, sent between the holder's wallets or received as an ordinary transfer | Keep with the holder |
| Verified StonkFun rewards paid in PUMP, from PENIS or other reward coins | Pledged in full while enrolled and collection is enabled |
| STONK or rewards paid in another mint | Excluded |
| Rewards paid before enrollment or before activation | Keep with the holder; no later collection |
| Rewards paid during a pause, stale-count stop or threshold suspension | Keep with the holder; no later collection |
| Opt-out | Stops subsequent contributions once the owner's transaction executes; no refund of completed contributions |
| Re-enrollment or resumption | Applies to future eligible payouts; never creates a debt for missed payouts |
| Direct treasury PENIS reaches 200 million | Holder contributions stop permanently; LP holdings do not count |

Activation requires a valid on-chain participation count at or above 30%. Once active, falling below 25% in the applicable on-chain state stops collection; returning to 30% restarts it. Exactly 25% does not deactivate an already active campaign. Counts remain sampled observations, not a continuous guarantee about every wallet balance.

Use the confirmed order of on-chain consent, activation, payout and exit transactions to resolve boundary races. A shared wall-clock timestamp or a treasury payout notification is not authorization. Each holder must be enrolled when their own payout executes, and the endowment must be eligible to collect at that time.

For this draft, “after activation” means payment execution time, consistent with the owner's request about when rewards are airdropped. A reward that accrued earlier but is first paid after activation is eligible. A reward that already landed in the holder's account before activation is excluded forever. Confirm that the distributor can distinguish first payment, retry and previously paid reward records.

## Preferred implementation

Have StonkFun call a routing instruction as part of its payout transaction. The instruction transfers PUMP from an authenticated distributor-owned source directly to the selected destination. It never uses the holder's PUMP account as a transfer source.

```text
Authenticated StonkFun payout
    -> validate distributor, reward identity, recipient and amount
    -> check recipient consent and current collection eligibility
    -> transfer from distributor to treasury if eligible
       otherwise transfer from distributor to recipient
```

The transaction must choose the destination using current on-chain state. A destination selected earlier by a server could be stale after an opt-out or pause. An ordinary SPL transfer directly to the treasury does not run these checks merely because the receiver is program-owned.

Routing from the distributor protects the holder's current and future purchases by construction: the program receives no authority to debit them. It also avoids an outstanding reward allowance that might accidentally consume unrelated deposits after a holder spends their reward.

This requires StonkFun's participation. A keeper cannot add an instruction to a transaction that StonkFun has already signed or confirmed without obtaining the required new signatures. Current-transaction instruction inspection is not a historical transaction proof. See Solana's [transaction model](https://solana.com/docs/core/transactions), [instruction introspection](https://solana.com/docs/core/instructions/instruction-introspection) and [token transfer authorization](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens).

## Proposed instruction requirements

The final ABI depends on the supported distributor integration. These are the required properties, not a claim that an adapter already exists:

1. Authenticate a distributor signer or its on-chain distribution program, with a fixed verified source policy. An observed wallet address alone is insufficient evidence of an official permanent integration. Do not add a project-operated signer that can declare arbitrary holder balances sweepable.
2. Bind the payout to the PUMP mint, intended recipient, raw amount, unique payout ID, distributor and endowment instance. The generating reward-coin mint is useful receipt metadata but is no longer an eligibility filter. Scope IDs across distributions and recipients so batch reuse cannot collide.
3. Validate the exact source token account, its distributor authority, the token program, the recipient's PUMP account, the endowment vaults and the applicable consent record. Reject a holder account substituted as the source. The caller must not choose an arbitrary payment destination.
4. Require a live owner-signed consent record for contribution routing. Owner-only exit must work while paused, stale, retired or complete. Revocation ordering must be visible to the routing instruction. Close/recreate cycles must not revive old claims or bypass payout replay protection.
5. Check actual direct treasury PENIS holdings and the permanent completion flag before routing. A payout that notices the goal can record completion while paying the holder normally; it must not transfer that holder's reward to the treasury.
6. Check activation, count freshness, guardian pause, retirement, token/market safety and the configured treasury capacity. Preserve existing protections instead of bypassing them through a new entry point.
7. Mark a payout consumed and record its actual destination atomically with the transfer, including payouts sent to the holder while inactive. A retry cannot later reinterpret an already-paid reward as a contribution. Failed transactions consume neither the payout nor its receipt.
8. Record recipient, payout ID, gross amount, net amount, destination, contribution amount and eligibility reason in an event. Credit only net funds actually received by the treasury; never infer a contribution from a scheduled job.

StonkFun remains trusted to identify and calculate its own reward payments, as it is today. The endowment must not gain the ability to pull purchased PUMP or invent a debt against holders if the distributor or keeper misbehaves. A distributor can still bypass the integration by paying normally; public reporting must distinguish consent from rewards actually contributed.

## Existing program changes required

- Replace or disable the legacy balance-above-baseline transfer path on-chain. Leaving it callable while only changing the website or keeper does not enforce the policy.
- Replace blanket PUMP spending approval with explicit routing consent. Adjust participation counting, refresh and pruning to recognize that consent. Specify an independent, owner-signed exit transaction and document how to submit it without the website. Existing participants, if any, must revoke old SPL approvals during migration.
- Remove baseline resets as a way to change contribution accounting. Historical baseline data must not become an amount owed under the new model.
- Integrate the new receipt and consent accounts, account sizes, rent funding and cleanup rules only after the supported StonkFun interface is confirmed. Keep replay protection after recipient exit or record closure.
- Update enrollment, exit, keeper jobs and user disclosures together. The keeper may monitor payouts and retry eligible distributor-authorized work; it must not become a trusted authority over withdrawal amounts.
- Keep future treasury-owned rewards separate from holder contribution gating: completion stops holder funding, while existing treasury funds and its own rewards retain the buy/liquidity policy.

## Open integration decisions

StonkFun must confirm that it can call this routing instruction or provide an equivalent authenticated on-chain payout mechanism. Obtain its supported signer/program identifiers, unique payout IDs, batching/retry behavior, costs and program-owned treasury eligibility. A public JSON endpoint or wallet label is not an on-chain authorization proof.

Public API inspection confirms that `/api/public/v1/rewards` connects generating-coin mints and reward mints to recent batch transaction signatures and amounts. These records can be joined to finalized transaction data for individual transfer amounts. The PENIS-specific `/tokens/{mint}/rewards` response provides aggregate totals. This is useful reconciliation evidence; it does not give this program a historical receipt proof or debit authorization. The documented recent-record limit is 100 with no published history cursor, so complete payout history requires additional support. A sampled API batch was reconciled to 17 on-chain STONK transfers with an exact amount match; that test does not establish coverage of all PENIS/PUMP payouts.

The exact capacity behavior remains a review decision. Recommended default: route only the amount allowed by the existing capacity bound and pay the remainder to the holder immediately, with no later recovery. This preserves the treasury cap and the rule against later wallet debits, but disclosures must explain that a 100% pledge can produce a smaller accepted contribution when safeguards limit collection. Do not silently remove the cap to satisfy a literal 100% collection claim.

If StonkFun cannot support direct routing, an authenticated claim mechanism requires a separate design review. A signed statement that a holder once received 100 PUMP is not sufficient to take 100 PUMP from that wallet later. Do not substitute a project-controlled oracle, timing heuristic or a “rewards-only wallet” recommendation and claim the same protection.

## Acceptance scenarios

These scenarios are requirements for future transaction tests; they are not implemented or passing tests yet.

| Scenario | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Enroll with 1,000 PUMP; receive 200 while waiting; buy 300; receive 100 after activation | Holder keeps 1,500 PUMP; contribution is at most the final 100, subject to safeguards |
| Eligible rewards from two StonkFun coins both pay PUMP | Both payouts are eligible; source-coin identity does not exclude either |
| A KNOWER reward pays STONK | No STONK moved or approved |
| Holder buys PUMP immediately before or after a payout | Purchase funds remain untouched under either transaction order |
| Holder spends existing PUMP before a later reward | No baseline refill suppresses the new eligible reward; no debit of other holder funds |
| Active threshold crosses 30%, falls to exactly 25%, then below 25%, then rises to 29% and 30% | Enable, remain enabled, suspend, remain suspended, resume, subject to other gates |
| Rewards paid before first activation, during a pause, or during a stale count | Paid to holder and permanently excluded from later collection |
| Holder opts out before payout, or payout executes before opt-out | Destination follows confirmed on-chain ordering, with no later debt |
| Rejoin after opting out or recreate the consent record | Only subsequent eligible payouts count; old payout IDs remain consumed |
| Repeat a payout ID, change its recipient, mint, instance or amount | Reject replay or mismatch; no double credit or duplicate transfer |
| Substitute an attacker signer, holder source account or foreign treasury | Reject before movement |
| Direct donation reaches the goal immediately before payout | Pay holder; persist completion; no contribution or future restart |
| Treasury reaches capacity or a health check blocks collection | Respect the bound; follow reviewed fallback; never leave a claim on the holder's balance |
| Token transfer or receipt creation fails | Entire transaction rolls back; a valid retry can proceed once |
| Program-owned treasury receives its own rewards after completion | Treasury income can continue without reopening holder contributions |

## Proposed disclosure after implementation

“While you are opted in and funding is active, you pledge your StonkFun rewards paid in PUMP, including rewards from other coins in the same wallet. PUMP you already hold, purchase or receive as an ordinary transfer is excluded. Rewards paid while funding is inactive stay yours and are never collected later. You can opt out at any time; the change takes effect when your transaction confirms. Holder contributions end permanently when the endowment directly holds 200 million PENIS.”

Publish this only after implementation and validation. Explain any safeguard-related collection limits alongside it. The current baseline contract and unlimited delegation do not support this disclosure.
